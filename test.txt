# Testcases

@ Policy Based Questions (RAG)
- What are your return policies?
- How long does shipping take?
- What payment methods do you accept?
- How can I reset my password?
- Can I cancel my order after it has been shipped?
- How do I contact customer support?

@ Structured Data Questions (Tool)
- What is the status of ORD1004?
- Show me the details of ORD1018.
- How much did I pay for ORD1002?
- What payment method was used for ORD1015?
- Which product was ordered in ORD1009?

@ Edge Cases:
What is the status of ORD9999?
Can I return ORD9999?
Do you insure pets?
Do you accept Bitcoin payments?
Can I return a spaceship?
